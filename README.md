Meridian Midnight is a top-down stealth heist game: plan your entry point, gadget and crew, then cross the Meridian Bank floor past four patrolling guards with vision cones and three sweeping CCTV cameras, grab gold bars and cash stacks, crack the vault for the Meridian Diamond, and slip out to the getaway van before the alarm locks the place down. Every run is a fresh puzzle of routes, timing and noise — drill loud and fast, pick the lock slow and silent, or blind the cameras with an EMP and walk straight through.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080.
